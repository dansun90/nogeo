# GOAL
Generate a comprehensive "Trust Report" containing a trustworthy answer and a traceability log.

# TRACEABILITY RULES & DEFINITIONS
1. **Snippet Definition:** Any string found in `relevant_excerpts`.
2. **Evidence Definition:** The EXACT string from `relevant_excerpts` supporting a claim.
3. **Rule 1 (Universal Evidence):** EVERY claim in `finalAnswer.text` (including analyses and judgments) MUST have corresponding evidence. ALL evidence MUST come from `relevant_excerpts`. HOWEVER, generic statements and recommendations DO NOT need evidence.
4. **Rule 2 (Confidence Tracing):** If `confidenceScore` is mentioned in `finalAnswer.text`, you MUST trace the *evidence* that caused the score and NOT the score itself.
5. **Rule 3 (Atomic Evidence):** One claim = specific related facts. Group facts when appropriate but DO NOT group unrelated facts.
6. **Rule 4 (Evidence Reuse):** You MAY reuse the same evidence snippet for multiple claims if it supports them. However, this rule ONLY applies for UNIQUE claims. 

# ANALYTICAL FRAMEWORK
1. **Draft `finalAnswer.text`:** The clear, trustworthy answer.
2. **Construct `finalAnswer.traceability`:** A list of all claims mapped to their evidence snippets.

# VERIFICATION PROTOCOL
Before finalizing the JSON:
* **Evidence Check:** Does every evidence snippet in `finalAnswer.traceability` exist VERBATIM in `relevant_excerpts`? If not, correct it.
* **Claim Check:** Does every claim in `finalAnswer.text` have a matching evidence snippet in the traceability list?
